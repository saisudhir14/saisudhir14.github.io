I mix these words up. Context and context window. Model and agent. Handoff and compaction. They sound alike, and I have been using them as if they meant the same thing.

[The AI Coding Dictionary](https://www.aicodingdictionary.com/) lists 71 of them in plain English, in seven groups. I used that list to start a [dictionary page](/dictionary/). The one-line definition under each of those words comes from that site. The note under the definition is for what I have actually seen, in Cursor, in Claude, and in the agents I ship at work.

That page is mine. The 71 words are the first group. I will add more groups as the work calls for them, including terms from the voice agents I build.

The notes are empty. When a word shows up in a real session, I will add the date and what happened.
