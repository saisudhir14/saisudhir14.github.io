Hi, I'm Sai. I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer at DaVita Kidney Care in Atlanta.

I take an idea and ship a working product in about four weeks. Backend, frontend, database, deployment. Whatever it takes.

Right now I'm building:

- A ride platform that gets kidney patients to their dialysis appointments
- An AI code review agent for our engineering teams
- An AI shift scheduler for clinics across the country

I work onsite with nurses and clinic staff, so I see how the software gets used. My team placed 3rd out of 64 in DaVita's 2026 hackathon.

Before this, I built a PII detection system in Go and Python at LexisNexis, and Voice AI apps in Go on GCP at TTEC. I started out at Virtusa building software for Lloyds Banking Group, then worked at Kagool and Bear Brown and Company.

I have a Master's in Information Systems from Central Michigan University.

Here I write about what I learn: AI agents, MCP, Go and cloud. Find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sudhirdontha/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/saisudhir14).
