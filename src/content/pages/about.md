Hello, my name is Sai. I am a Forward Deployed Engineer at DaVita Kidney Care in Atlanta. I work with the innovation and executive teams to turn their ideas into working products, usually within four weeks. I build from 0 to 1: backend, frontend, database and deployment, whatever it takes to ship.

Right now I am building a non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) platform that lets kidney care patients schedule, manage and track rides to their dialysis appointments. It works with NEMT brokers such as ModivCare to handle booking, eligibility and recurring trips. I also lead a team building an in-house code review agent with Python, FastAPI, GCP, LLM APIs, React and PostgreSQL, and I work on an AI-powered shift scheduling app for clinical teams at facilities across the country. Much of this happens onsite, next to clinical staff, facility administrators, RNs and PCTs. In DaVita's 2026 hackathon, my team placed 3rd out of 64 with a prototype for patient admission and placement.

Before DaVita, I was a Software Systems Engineer III at LexisNexis Risk Solutions. I built a PII detection system in Python and Go that scans text and files, and uses GitHub webhooks and the Checks API to catch sensitive data in commits and pull requests.

Before that, I spent over a year at TTEC as a Software Engineer on Voice AI. I worked on low-latency voice applications in Go on GCP and integrated ASR, TTS and translation providers (Google Cloud, DeepL, Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Cartesia) behind one common interface, so we could switch providers without changing application code. I also set up observability with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus and Grafana, and built HIPAA-compliant data loss prevention with Google Cloud DLP.

I started my career at Virtusa, working on projects for Lloyds Banking Group, and then worked as a full stack engineer at Kagool. After my Master's, I joined Bear Brown and Company as a software engineer. That early client work taught me how to gather requirements, turn business problems into technical plans and deliver on time. I enjoy working where product and customer meet, whether that means building the solution, demoing it to stakeholders or debugging production at 2am. I have also won public speaking and pitch competitions, and I think clear communication is part of good engineering.

I have a Master's in Information Systems from Central Michigan University and a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Swami Vivekananda Institute of Technology.

On this blog I write about what I learn: AI agents, MCP, context and harness engineering, Go and cloud. You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sudhirdontha/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/saisudhir14).
